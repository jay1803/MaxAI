# Drafts for review

Anything other people will read is a draft for the owner: chat messages and
replies, emails, comments in shared tools, documents for colleagues, in-app
copy, and any message to a person or a bot in a shared channel.

## The rule

1. Prepare the draft in place: in the chat tool's composer, as an email draft,
   or as a clearly marked unpublished document.
2. Stop. Do not send, publish, share or notify.
3. Set the issue to In Review. First line: what to send, to whom, and how the
   issue completes after sending.

System actions with no prose (granting access by procedure, ticking a
checklist item) stay whitelisted.

## Before showing a draft

Run this checklist. It comes from comparing many agent drafts with what the
owner actually sent: the owner cut most drafts and lengthened none, and the
sent text was roughly 60% of the draft.

- [ ] The first line answers the ask.
- [ ] Every sentence the reader does not need is deleted, including background
      they already have and the agent's own process.
- [ ] At most two questions, each answerable in one word or as an either-or
      choice, without a reasoning paragraph.
- [ ] No placeholder, no commitment the owner did not make, no date the owner
      did not give.
- [ ] One topic per message.
- [ ] Plain layout for short messages: short lines, no headings, no bold.
- [ ] The team's own terms, not the agent's.
- [ ] Only facts the owner can stand behind; inference is marked.
- [ ] Before drafting, the literal question from the source was copied into
      notes, and the draft answers that sentence with its scope words intact.
- [ ] Length under about 60% of the first version.

## Mechanics that bite

- Check for an existing draft in the same place before writing. Writing into a
  composer can replace a draft the owner started.
- A draft written from a web session may not show in the owner's open desktop
  client. Say so when that was not checked.
- An unsent draft is not proof of pending work. The owner often deletes the
  agent's draft and writes a shorter one. Check before referring to it.
- A sent message is the only record of what went out. Read it, not the draft.

## Disclosure

When a message is sent on the owner's behalf by an agent, it says so inside
the message, in plain words, and carries a short disclosure tag.

## Measuring quality

For each draft, record whether it was sent unchanged and the similarity
between the draft and the sent text. Track both weekly. A category that
reaches several unchanged sends becomes a proposal to the owner to let the
agent send it directly, never an automatic change.
